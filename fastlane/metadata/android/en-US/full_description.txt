Yomira is an anime, series and movie player for Android phones, with a liquid glass interface and a player you can tune for smoothness and picture quality. Catalogs come from extensions you install from repositories you choose.
<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Liquid glass interface: floating tab bar, glass top bars, a Home tab with highlights and a streaming-style details page.</li>
<li>A player built on mpv with smoothness options, a per-video optimizer, a heat guard and a premium layout.</li>
<li>Real-time Anime4K, color correction and color grading.</li>
<li>Offline Anime4K: Enhanced Downloads and Smart Anime4K.</li>
<li>Discord Rich Presence through the Discord app, without logging in.</li>
<li>Episode names and artwork from TMDB, and skip intro without tracking.</li>
<li>Trackers: AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori, Simkl, Trakt and Bangumi.</li>
<li>Categories, downloads, backups and library updates.</li>
<li>No telemetry, no ads, no accounts.</li>
</ul>
