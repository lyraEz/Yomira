The tab bar

A lens that glides between tabs

The floating capsule carries a glass lens that slides on a spring and stretches as it moves. Scroll down and the bar shrinks to its icons; scroll back up and it returns.

  • Tap or drag along the bar to change tabs.
  • Scroll the phone to watch it shrink and grow.
  • Same springs as the app: stiffness 420, damping 0.66 for the lens and 0.82 for the bar.

Player

Made for watching

Built on mpv and tuned for phones, from budget models to flagships.

  • Premium layout with the title at the bottom and a thin progress bar.
  • "You are watching" screen when you pause, with the episode's name and synopsis.
  • Smoothness: match the display to the video, motion interpolation and image quality.
  • Heat guard that switches to lighter settings when the phone warms up.
  • Tools: A-B loop, frame step, rotation, speed per anime, sleep at the end.
  • Sound: night mode, clear dialogue, spatial audio, balance.
Picture

Sharper lines, your colors

Real-time Anime4K with a recommendation for your phone, plus offline processing for the best result.

  • Enhanced Downloads: choose Original or Anime4K when downloading; the sharper copy plays first and the original is kept.
  • Smart Anime4K: prepares the episode you opened, and optionally the next, in the background.
  • Color correction and grading: temperature, tint, exposure, shadows, highlights, vibrance and looks like Cinema, Vintage or Black and white.

Discord Rich Presence

Show what you watch. No login.

Yomira talks to the Discord app installed on your phone, so your profile shows a polished card while you watch.

  • Never asks for your Discord token, password or email.
  • Filled from TMDB: clean anime names, episode titles and covers.
  • Configurable: lines, images, progress or elapsed time, up to two buttons, and which extensions and categories appear.
  • Private when you want: incognito mode and hidden anime never show.
Guide

From install to first episode

A few minutes, once. After that, Yomira keeps itself up to date.

Install

  1. Download the APK below. Most phones need arm64-v8a; if unsure, take universal.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Yomira and follow the setup screens.

Updates install over the current version and keep your library. Never uninstall to update.

Add a repository

  1. Go to Browse › Extensions › ⋮ › Extension repos.
  2. Paste the address of a repository. It ends with index.min.json.
  3. Yomira checks it before saving and explains what is wrong if it cannot be read.

Yomira does not host or recommend content. Use repositories you trust.

Install and watch

  1. In Browse › Extensions, install an extension and confirm if Android asks.
  2. Its sources appear in Browse › Sources. Search, open an anime and press Play.
  3. In the player, swipe for brightness and volume and double-tap to seek. Filters holds quality, smoothness and color.

Discord

  1. Install the Discord app on the phone and sign in.
  2. In Yomira: Settings › Connections › Discord, turn it on.
  3. Tap Test on Discord to see a sample card for 30 seconds, then shape the card to your taste.

Anime4K offline

  1. Open Settings › Anime4K and run the speed test to see how long your phone needs.
  2. Turn on Enhanced Downloads. Downloads will ask: Original or Anime4K, with mode and quality.
  3. Follow progress in the processing queue. By default it only runs while charging.

Color

  1. While watching, open Filters.
  2. Color correction fixes the picture: temperature, tint, exposure, shadows, highlights, vibrance.
  3. Color grading gives it a look: pick Cinema, Warm, Vintage and more, then fine-tune.

Updates and betas

  1. More › About › Check for updates shows new versions with the right APK for your phone.
  2. Turn on Beta versions in the same screen to try new features first.

Backups

  1. More › Settings › Data and storage › Create backup saves library, history, categories, settings and repositories.
  2. Restore it from the same screen on a new phone, then reinstall extensions from the restored repositories.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Yomira free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does Yomira come with anime?

No. Yomira hosts no content. Catalogs come from extensions you install from repositories you choose.

Does the Discord feature need my token?

No. It talks to the Discord app on your phone through Discord's own SDK. Yomira never asks for or stores your login, password or token.

Which phones are supported?

Android 8.0 or newer on 64-bit (arm64-v8a) or 32-bit (armeabi-v7a) ARM phones. The glass effects adapt to each phone.

Will updating erase my library?

No. Every version uses the same package and signature, so it installs over the previous one and keeps everything.

What are beta versions?

Test builds published before a stable release. Turn on Beta versions in More › About to be offered them, or get them from the Yomira-releases repository.

Why does offline Anime4K take so long?

It runs Anime4K's neural network shaders on every frame of the episode on your phone's GPU. The speed test tells you exactly how long your phone needs.
